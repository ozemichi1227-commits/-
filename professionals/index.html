For Healthcare Professionals

This section is intended for physicians and other healthcare professionals. It summarizes clinical trial activity, treatment context, and useful resources related to drug therapy for intractable vascular tumors and vascular malformations. For full detail — including the latest literature reviews and academic meeting reports — please refer to the medical section of the Japanese official site.

Clinical trials (SIVA)

Our group has conducted a series of investigator-initiated clinical trials — collectively referred to as SIVA (Sirolimus for Intractable Vascular Anomalies) — evaluating the efficacy and safety of sirolimus (NPC-12T) in patients with intractable vascular tumors and vascular malformations. These multicenter trials, run in collaboration with hospitals across Japan, provided the evidence base for the Japanese regulatory approvals of sirolimus in this field.

Regulatory milestones in Japan

YearMilestone
2021 Approval of sirolimus (Rapalimus tablets) for intractable lymphatic anomalies: lymphatic malformation, lymphangiomatosis, Gorham–Stout disease, and lymphangiectasia.
Jan 2024 Expanded indication to intractable vascular tumors and vascular malformations (including KHE and related tumors, venous malformation / blue rubber bleb nevus syndrome, and combined malformations such as Klippel–Trenaunay syndrome), plus approval of a granule formulation for pediatric use.
Approval status and labelling are specific to Japan and current as of the dates shown. Clinicians outside Japan should consult their own national regulatory information and product labelling.

Diagnostic and treatment context

  • Classification: Diagnosis follows the ISSVA classification, separating vascular tumors from vascular malformations and, among malformations, high-flow from slow-flow lesions.
  • Molecular basis: Many lesions harbor somatic variants activating the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway (e.g. PIK3CA) or the RAS/MAPK pathway, providing the rationale for mTOR inhibition.
  • Multidisciplinary care: Management typically integrates pediatrics, dermatology, plastic/reconstructive surgery, interventional radiology, hematology/ oncology, and other specialties.
  • Monitoring on sirolimus: Therapeutic drug monitoring of trough concentrations, along with surveillance for stomatitis, infection, metabolic changes, cytopenias, and the rare risk of interstitial lung disease.

International conferences

The ISSVA World Congress is the principal international meeting in this field; the 2026 congress was held in Philadelphia, USA (May 19–22, 2026). Our group participates in international scientific exchange on classification, genetics, and drug therapy of vascular anomalies.
