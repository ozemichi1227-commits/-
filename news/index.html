News

Selected updates from the research group. For the full and most current list of announcements, please see the news section of the Japanese official site.

  • 2026 English site The English version of this website has been launched to make information on intractable vascular anomalies and sirolimus accessible to an international audience.
  • May 2026 Conference The ISSVA (International Society for the Study of Vascular Anomalies) World Congress was held in Philadelphia, USA, on May 19–22, 2026.
  • Jan 2024 For patients Sirolimus (Rapalimus tablets) received an additional approved indication in Japan for intractable vascular tumors and vascular malformations, and a new granule formulation was approved — making the medicine easier for infants and young children to take.
  • 2021 Research Sirolimus was approved in Japan for intractable lymphatic anomalies (lymphatic malformation, lymphangiomatosis, Gorham–Stout disease, and lymphangiectasia), based on the group’s investigator-initiated clinical trials.
  • 2021 Research The group’s research results were featured in AMED’s annual collection of research achievements.
Dates and details of some entries are summarized for the English site. Please refer to the Japanese official site for authoritative, detailed announcements.
