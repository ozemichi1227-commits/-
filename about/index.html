About This Site

Who operates this site

This website is created and operated by the research group on drug therapy for intractable vascular tumors and vascular malformations (the AMED Ozeki Group, also known as the Sirolimus Research Group). The group is led by Dr. Michio Ozeki, Department of Pediatrics, Gifu University Hospital, and is funded by the Japan Agency for Medical Research and Development (AMED) for research on the development of molecular targeted therapy using sirolimus for intractable vascular tumors and vascular malformations.

The original Japanese site is available at https://cure-vas.jp/. This English version was prepared to make the same kind of information accessible to patients, families, and healthcare professionals outside Japan.

Our goals

  • To provide the latest information on drug therapy for vascular tumors and vascular malformations — especially sirolimus — in a way that is easy for patients, families, and healthcare professionals to understand.
  • To help newly diagnosed patients and their families find accurate information and reduce anxiety.
  • To share information about clinical trials and research in Japan and overseas, specialized treatment facilities, patient organizations, and financial support systems for medical care.

How the content is prepared

The content of this site is written and reviewed by physicians and researchers of the research group. In addition, the site is developed with the cooperation of Patient and Public Involvement (PPI) members — patients and family members who review the content from the reader’s point of view and help us keep it clear, accurate, and useful. Learn more on the Research Group & PPI page.

Medical disclaimer: The information on this site is provided for general educational purposes. It is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it may not reflect the approval status or standard of care in your country. Always consult your physician or another qualified health provider about your individual situation.

Contact

For inquiries about this site or the research group, please use the contact information on the Japanese official site. Please note that we cannot provide individual medical consultations or second opinions through this website.
